Solo

Solo games are played by one player against the computer. Simply go to the game's tab to play.

Local

Local games are played by two players on the same device. Simply go to the game's tab to play.

Multi

Multi games are played by two players on different devices over the internet. To play, Host a game, input who you want to play along with their account information. Then you will be given a message and senders. Whenever you are prompted to email, open up your indbox of choice and email to the provided recipents
